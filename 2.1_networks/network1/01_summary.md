The goal of this lab is to learn how to design, build, and configure a computer network using networking devices such as routers, switches, and computers. It helps us understand how devices communicate and share information over a network.
We are expected to create a small network (LAN – Local Area Network) by connecting multiple computers to switches and routers, configuring IP addresses, and ensuring that all devices can communicate with each other successfully.
